Privacy

Shiori 栞 is a private, non-commercial tool for reading and learning Japanese. Last updated: November 2026.

By default, everything stays on your device

Without signing in, your vocabulary, review progress, read marks and settings are stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. No ads, no analytics, no tracking code.

If you sign in to sync

  • What is stored: your email address and your sync data (vocabulary, review progress, read marks and settings). No name, phone number or location.
  • Where: Supabase servers in Dublin, Ireland (EU).
  • Who can read it: you, through the app. The maintainer can technically access the database. Sync data is not encrypted on your device before upload.
  • Password: optional. If you set one, Supabase Auth stores it as a hash, never in plain text. Otherwise you sign in with a code sent to your email.
  • Email: used only to send login codes.
  • Deleting: “Delete account & cloud data” in Settings removes your account and cloud data right away. Data on your device stays.

Outside services

  • Google Fonts: the app loads its fonts from Google when you open it, so Google receives your IP address and browser information, as with any site that uses Google Fonts.
  • Japanese voice: most read-aloud audio is pre-generated and included with the app. Any sentence without a pre-generated file is read by your device’s or browser’s built-in Japanese voice; depending on your platform, that speech may be provided by Apple or Google.
  • News links: the “Today’s news” section links to outside news sites (NHK, Asahi, Mainichi and others). Their own privacy policies apply once you open them.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app and the daily texts.

隱私說明（繁體中文）

栞 Shiori 是私人維護、非商業的日語閱讀與單字學習工具。最後更新：2026 年 11 月

預設：一切都只在你的裝置上

不登入的話，你的單字本、複習進度、已讀記錄和設定只保存在這台裝置的瀏覽器裡。不會上傳。沒有廣告，沒有統計或追蹤程式碼。

開啟電子郵件登入同步時

  • 存什麼：你的電子郵件地址，以及同步資料（單字本、複習進度、已讀記錄和設定）。不收集姓名、電話或位置。
  • 存在哪裡：Supabase 的歐盟伺服器（愛爾蘭都柏林）。
  • 誰能看到：只有你能透過 app 讀寫自己的資料。維護者在技術上可以存取資料庫。同步資料上傳前不會在你的裝置上加密。
  • 密碼：可選。設定密碼後，Supabase Auth 只保存它的雜湊值，不會明文儲存。不設密碼的話，用寄到信箱的驗證碼登入。
  • 郵件：只用來寄送登入驗證碼。
  • 刪除：在「設定」裡點「刪除帳號與雲端資料」，帳號和雲端資料會立刻刪除。裝置上的資料不受影響。

會連接的外部服務

  • Google 字型：開啟 app 時會從 Google 載入字型，因此 Google 會收到你的 IP 位址和瀏覽器資訊，與其他使用 Google 字型的網站相同。
  • 日語語音：大部分朗讀語音已預先生成並隨 app 提供。沒有預先生成檔案的句子，會由裝置或瀏覽器內建的日語語音朗讀；依平台不同，這些語音可能由蘋果或谷歌提供。
  • 新聞連結：「今天的新聞」會連到外部新聞網站（NHK、朝日、每日新聞等）。打開後，適用各網站自己的隱私政策。
  • GitHub Pages：app 本身和每日文章放在這裡。
